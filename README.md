# Research-Proposal
## Research Topic

My research explores the effectiveness of Virtual Reality (VR) in computer science education, focusing on teaching sorting algorithms through immersive and interactive learning environments. The study aims to compare VR-based learning with traditional teaching methods to evaluate differences in student understanding, engagement, learning speed, and knowledge retention. Data will be collected through student assessments, surveys, questionnaires, and interviews. With my background in Information Technology and programming projects, I aim to investigate how VR can improve the learning experience and make complex algorithmic concepts easier to understand.
